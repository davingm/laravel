# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want davingm/laravel to be a welcoming, respectful place to learn, build, and collaborate. People participating in this project are expected to treat one another with care, regardless of experience level, identity, background, or preferred way of communicating.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Welcome questions and feedback from people who are new to the project.
- Discuss ideas and code; do not attack or demean people.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct harm caused by your actions.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, personal attacks, sexualized attention without consent, doxxing, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable. This applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, project events, and other spaces where someone is representing or participating in this project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a conduct concern, contact the maintainer privately through the contact options on the [project maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/davingm). Do not post sensitive details in a public issue or pull request. Include links, dates, relevant context, and what outcome would help, if you can safely provide them.

The maintainer will review reports as impartially and confidentially as possible, taking the reporter's safety and privacy into account. Depending on the circumstances, enforcement may include a warning, removal of harmful content, a temporary restriction, or exclusion from project spaces. Retaliation against someone for raising a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces and to public interactions where someone is acting on behalf of or materially affecting the project community.
